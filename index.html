Why Your Routine Changed (Same 4 Days)

Comparing your old 15-move circuit to the recomposition Upper/Lower + Zone 2 plan

Added (Biggest Gaps)
  • Chest Pressing (Mon & Fri): Old plan had 0 chest exercises. Added Incline Press + Flat Chest Press.
  • Shoulders & V-Taper (Mon & Fri): Old plan had 0 shoulder work. Added Overhead Press + Lateral Raises to widen shoulders above the waist.
  • 2–3 Hard Sets per Move: Upgraded from 1 heavy set to 10–14 weekly sets/muscle at 1–2 reps shy of failure.
Kept & Reordered
  • Leg Press moved to #1 (Wed): Replaces late-workout Goblet Squats so you lift heavy legs while fresh.
  • Abduction & Adduction moved to end (Wed): Kept as separate machines at the end instead of pre-fatiguing hips first.
  • Kept favorites: Pull-ups/Chin-ups, Seated Row, Lying Leg Curl, Leg Extension, Calf Raise, Glute Kickback, DB Curls, Tricep Machine, Rope Crunch, Captain's Chair.
Replaced for Love-Handle Fat Loss
  • 4-Mile Post-Lift Run → 20–35 min Zone 2 Incline Walk: Same 4 gym visits, far less leg interference and post-run hunger rebound so your calorie deficit actually sticks.
  • Dropped Torso Twist Machine: Spot reduction is a myth; heavy oblique twisting builds thicker side waist muscle under love handles without burning fat there.
  • Nutrition Anchor: Pair this with a 10–20% calorie deficit and 0.8–1.0 g protein/lb daily.
